what I do

I offer comprehensive penetration testing services designed to identify, assess, and validate security vulnerabilities across systems, applications, and organizational environments. Through practical security assessments and adversarial testing methodologies, I help organizations uncover weaknesses, understand their potential impact, and strengthen their overall security posture.

Technical penetration testing
Assessment of external and internal infrastructure to identify vulnerabilities, misconfigurations, and exploitable weaknesses. This includes network reconnaissance, service enumeration, vulnerability validation, exploitation, privilege escalation, Active Directory security, and post-exploitation analysis. Testing is focused on understanding how individual weaknesses can be chained into broader compromises.
Web application security
Security assessments of authenticated and unauthenticated applications, APIs, and supporting technologies. Testing covers injection vulnerabilities, authentication mechanisms, session management, access control, insecure business logic, and server-side weaknesses. Findings are validated to establish their practical impact.
Social engineering
Authorized assessments of human-centered security controls and organizational processes. This includes realistic phishing scenarios, pretexting, telephone-based assessments, and help-desk security testing. Engagements evaluate how effectively personnel and established procedures withstand social engineering attempts.
Physical security
On-site assessments of physical access controls and facility security. Testing evaluates entry barriers, access restrictions, and the effectiveness of physical security measures. Alarm systems, surveillance, and other detection mechanisms are assessed within the agreed engagement scope.
